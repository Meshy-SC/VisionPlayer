Privacy Policy — Meta Horizon Store

Effective date: July 25, 2026 · Last updated: July 25, 2026

The Short Version (VRC.Privacy Compliant)

VisionPlayer for Meta Horizon OS does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal data. The application operates entirely on your Meta Quest headset. There are no user accounts, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. All playback preferences, history, and SMB credentials remain encrypted on your device.

1. Scope & Application

This Privacy Policy applies to VisionPlayer for Meta Horizon OS distributed through the Meta Horizon Store for Meta Quest headsets (Quest 2, Quest Pro, Quest 3, and Quest 3S). It addresses Meta's Virtual Reality Checks for privacy (VRC.Privacy.1 through VRC.Privacy.4).

2. Data We Do Not Collect (VRC.Privacy.2)

We believe your personal media browsing should remain completely private. VisionPlayer does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, Meta user ID)
  • Device serial numbers or hardware advertising identifiers
  • Location data (neither precise GPS nor coarse IP-based location)
  • App usage analytics, performance telemetry, or crash logs sent to remote servers
  • The filenames, video content, folder paths, or playback habits from your library
  • Biometric sensor data (hand tracking coordinates or spatial mesh data)

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide seamless video playback and remember your preferences, VisionPlayer stores minimal app state locally on your Meta Quest headset using standard Android storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences and internal database). This data includes:

  • Playback resume positions per video file
  • Favorites list and recent playback history
  • Per-video format overrides (projection type, 3D mode, aspect ratio, audio track selection)
  • Saved SMB network share bookmarks
  • Encrypted SMB login credentials (stored using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences with hardware keystore protection)

4. Network Activity & SMB Streaming

VisionPlayer initiates local network and internet connections only in response to your explicit actions:

  • SMB Local Network Streaming: When you connect to a PC, Mac, or NAS on your Wi-Fi network, VisionPlayer connects directly to your local file server over port 445 (SMB) to stream video. This communication occurs strictly inside your local area network and is never routed to external servers.
  • Direct HTTP/HLS Streams: If you enter a custom video stream URL, the app connects directly to that URL to fetch media data.

5. System Permissions (VRC.Privacy.4)

VisionPlayer requests only the system permissions necessary for core video playback functionality:

  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required for streaming video over your local network (SMB) or loading user-provided web URLs.
  • Hand Tracking (HAND_TRACKING): Allows you to interact with Horizon OS panels, buttons, and transport controls using natural hand gestures. Hand tracking input is processed entirely by Horizon OS; VisionPlayer does not record or store hand images or skeletal data.
  • Audio Settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Allows the player's transport controls to adjust volume during playback.

6. Third-Party Services & Ads

VisionPlayer contains no third-party advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no analytics frameworks. We do not share, sell, or license any user information to third parties.

7. User Control & Data Deletion (VRC.Privacy.3)

Because all data created by VisionPlayer resides locally on your Meta Quest headset:

  • You can clear all app preferences, history, and stored SMB credentials at any time by going to Settings > Storage > VisionPlayer > Clear Data on your Meta Quest.
  • Uninstalling VisionPlayer permanently removes all stored app preferences and credentials from your headset.
  • Because no data is stored on remote servers, there is no remote data retention or need for external data deletion requests.

8. Children's Privacy

VisionPlayer is a general-audience video player. It does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13.
