Vision Player Pro Privacy Policy — Android XR (Google Play)

App: Vision Player Pro, on Google Play for Android XR devices · Publisher: Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
Effective date: the date of the app’s first release on Google Play · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary

Vision Player Pro has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Your videos, history, settings and passwords stay on your device. We cannot see them, and the app sends nothing to us.

The only other company involved is Google, which runs Android XR and Google Play. When you open the app, it checks with Google Play that this copy was bought on Google Play (section 2.1).

1. Who We Are

Vision Player Pro (“the app”) is published on Google Play by Meshy. The person responsible for your data (the data controller) is Ahmed Dalhi, based in France.

Contact: davinci.dalhi@gmail.com

This policy covers Vision Player Pro on Android XR devices (for example XR headsets and wired XR glasses). The Meta Quest version has its own policy.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

We, the developer, do not receive any data from the app.

2.1 Ownership check (Google Play)

Google Play adds a check to the copy of the app you download (“automatic protection”), which confirms that it was installed from Google Play; if it wasn’t, Google Play offers to get the app from Google Play. This check is run by Google Play. We do not receive your Google account or any other information from it. Google handles it under Google’s Privacy Policy.

2.2 Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you.

3. Data That Stays on Your Device

To remember your library and preferences, the app saves the following on your device. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

  • Playback resume positions
  • Playback history and favorites
  • Per-video format settings (projection, 3D mode, audio and subtitle choices)
  • Network server bookmarks
  • Network server passwords, encrypted with Android EncryptedSharedPreferences, which uses hardware-backed Android Keystore protection
  • Thumbnails of your videos, and a fingerprint of each video file the app has shown, with the file’s location. The fingerprint is calculated from the file’s size and its first and last 64 KB. It lets the app recognise a video you have moved or renamed, so the video keeps its thumbnail, resume position, favorite and history. If a video disappears from its folder, these are kept for up to 180 days in case it comes back.

Android backup. If you have turned on backup in your device settings, Android may include the items above in your device backup, which Google stores for you, so your library follows you to a new device. Network server passwords and thumbnails are excluded from backup.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

Vision Player Pro does not collect:

  • Your name, email address or phone number (unless you email us, as in section 2.2)
  • Your Google account or any account identifier
  • Hardware serial numbers or advertising identifiers
  • Location, whether precise or approximate
  • Usage analytics, telemetry or crash reports
  • Video names, video content, folder paths or viewing habits
  • Biometric data, such as hand-tracking, eye-tracking or camera data

5. Where Your Data Is Stored and Who Can Access It

  • Vision Player Pro has no servers, no database and no user accounts of its own.
  • The data in section 3 is stored only on your device (and in your own device backup, if you turned it on).
  • We do not use any data processors, service providers or partners that can access your data.

6. How Long We Keep Data

  • Data on your device (section 3): until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send us: while we handle your request (section 2.2).

7. Requesting Data Deletion

All users, in every country, can delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.

Data on your device

Everything the app stores is on your device. To delete it:

  1. Open your device’s Settings > Apps > Vision Player Pro > Storage, and choose Clear storage; or
  2. Uninstall the app.

Anything else

We hold no other data about you. If you emailed us and want that email deleted, write to davinci.dalhi@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

8. Your Other Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email davinci.dalhi@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, free of charge.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In France, that is the CNIL (cnil.fr). Elsewhere, contact your local authority.

Legal basis (for users in the EEA and the UK): the ownership check protects the app from unlicensed copies (our legitimate interest); we use your emails because you asked us something (also our legitimate interest).

9. Network Activity

The app connects to the network only for the following:

  • Local network SMB streaming: connects directly over port 445 to a PC, Mac or NAS that you add, and streams from it over your local Wi-Fi. This traffic stays inside your local network. Your login for that computer is sent only to that computer.
  • DLNA media servers: sends a discovery request to your local network (SSDP multicast) to find DLNA/UPnP media servers, then streams directly from the one you choose. This traffic stays inside your local network.
  • Automatic share discovery: listens for file shares that computers and NAS devices announce on your local network (mDNS), so they appear without typing an address.
  • Google Play: the ownership check in section 2.1.

10. System Permissions

  • Internet and network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used for local SMB and DLNA streaming and the Google Play ownership check.
  • Local network discovery (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): used to discover DLNA media servers on your local network, only while a search is running. These permissions do not give the app your location or Wi-Fi network details.
  • Videos on your device (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): lets the Files tab list and play the videos stored on your device — all of them, or only the ones you choose to share with the app. The app reads video files only, never photos, audio or documents.
  • Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): added by the Android media playback library (Media3), so the device does not sleep in the middle of a video.

The app uses Android XR’s hand tracking only through the system’s standard input; it does not request or receive hand, eye or camera data.

11. Third Parties, Advertising and Sharing

Vision Player Pro contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone.

12. Children

Vision Player Pro is a general-audience video player. It never asks for personal details and handles the same limited data for every user, whatever their age.

13. Security

We keep no copy of your data on any server. Network server passwords on your device are encrypted with hardware-backed Android Keystore protection.

14. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here and update the “Last updated” date above. If a change affects what data the app collects, we will also say so in the app’s release notes.
